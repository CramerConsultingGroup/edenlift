Secure Digital Signing

Our digital signature platform provides a secure, legally-binding method for signing and verifying documents electronically.

Features

🔒 Blockchain Verified

All signatures are recorded on blockchain for immutable proof.

⚡ Fast & Easy

Sign documents in seconds from any device.

📜 Legally Binding

Compliant with e-signature regulations worldwide.

🔍 Audit Trail

Complete history of all document actions and signatures.

How It Works

  1. Upload Document: Upload the document you need signed or select from templates
  2. Add Signers: Specify who needs to sign and their email addresses
  3. Sign: Signers receive a secure link to review and sign electronically
  4. Verify: All parties receive a verified copy with blockchain proof

Document Types

Our signature platform supports various document types:

  • Investment agreements and contracts
  • Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs)
  • Partnership agreements
  • Grant applications and funding documents
  • Consent forms and waivers
  • Custom document templates

Security & Compliance

We take document security seriously:

  • Encryption: End-to-end encryption for all documents
  • Authentication: Multi-factor authentication required for signing
  • Standards: Compliant with ESIGN Act, UETA, and eIDAS regulations
  • Storage: Secure cloud storage with regular backups
  • Privacy: GDPR compliant with data privacy protections
